DFW Airport to AT&T Stadium Transportation: A Fly-In Fan's Game-Day Playbook

July 26, 2026 · 9 min read · Purple Heart Limo

Flying in for a Cowboys game is one of the best trips in the NFL — but the moment you land at DFW, the clock starts. AT&T Stadium sits about 25–35 minutes from DFW International and roughly the same from Dallas Love Field, and game-day traffic can double that. The fix is simple: book a flat-rate chauffeur who tracks your flight, waits free for 60 minutes, and knows the Arlington back routes. This is your playbook for turning a plane ticket into a gate-side arrival with Purple Heart Limo — luggage, hotel, kickoff timing and all.

How Far Is AT&T Stadium From the Airports?

Both major DFW-area airports feed into Arlington easily, but the drive time swings hard on game day. Plan for the high end:

FromOff-Peak DriveGame-Day Drive
DFW International Airport25–35 min45–70 min
Dallas Love Field25–35 min45–70 min
Downtown Dallas~20 min40+ min

The reason the numbers double: Collins Street and Randol Mill Road jam worst as kickoff approaches, and stadium lots open four hours early. A local chauffeur routes around the worst of it — a self-driving rental fan does not.

Flight-Timing Strategy: Land With Room to Spare

Cowboys home games usually kick at noon or 3:25pm (with the occasional Thursday or Sunday-night primetime slot). Build your inbound flight around the traffic, not the clock on paper:

  • For a noon kickoff: Land the night before if you can. A same-morning arrival leaves no cushion once game-day traffic and a hotel check-in stack up.
  • For a 3:25pm kickoff: Aim to be on the ground by late morning — roughly noon at the latest — so you can drop bags, and still reach the stadium before the pre-game crush.
  • For a Thursday-night game (like the Oct 8, 2026 Buccaneers matchup): an afternoon arrival works, but rush-hour plus game traffic is a double-hit, so pad an extra 30 minutes.
  • Always cushion for delays: a missed connection shouldn't cost you kickoff.

Not sure which airport to fly into? Our DFW Airport car service and Love Field car service pages both feed straight into Arlington, so book whichever flight is cheaper or better-timed.

A quick note on the two airports: DFW International is the bigger hub with the most direct routes from out-of-state, while Love Field is smaller, closer to central Dallas, and often quicker to get out of. For a Cowboys weekend either works well since both sit roughly the same distance from Arlington. If you're weighing a connection through a hub versus a nonstop into Love Field, factor in that a smaller airport means faster baggage claim and a quicker curb — which matters most on a tight noon-kickoff morning.

Luggage & Hotel Logistics

Nobody wants to lug a suitcase into AT&T Stadium — and you can't. Sequence your day so you land clean:

  • Hotel drop first: If your flight lands with time, have your chauffeur run bags to your hotel before the stadium. Many fans stay in the Arlington Entertainment District, walkable to the gates.
  • Direct-to-stadium only if late: If you're tight on time, stow carry-ons in the vehicle and head straight in — but remember the stadium's clear-bag policy limits what you can carry.
  • One vehicle, one driver, all weekend: Keep the same chauffeur for the airport, the game, dinner, and the return flight so nothing gets juggled.

If you're building the full weekend around the district, our Arlington Entertainment District game-day itinerary maps the best pre-game and post-game stops within a short walk of your hotel.

One more logistics tip: AT&T Stadium enforces a clear-bag policy, so anything you can't carry inside — camera bags, jackets, a change of shoes — is best left in the vehicle rather than a distant rental car. With a chauffeur, your "trunk" travels with you all day and is waiting the moment you walk out, which is a small thing that makes a big difference on a long game day.

  • 4–6 fans: Luxury SUV (Escalade) — plenty of room for people and roller bags.
