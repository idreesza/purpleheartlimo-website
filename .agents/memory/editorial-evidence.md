---
name: Editorial evidence boundaries
description: How to distinguish existing marketing claims from verified business facts during content revisions
---

Do not treat a repeated claim in existing articles as independent verification of a business policy or operating capability. When service and rate pages conflict, obtain business confirmation or omit the specific promise; do not choose the more generous version.

**Why:** Content review found contradictory airport waiting allowances and gratuity wording, while articles also represented a quote request as a guaranteed reservation. Copying these claims into new guides would perpetuate the uncertainty.

**How to apply:** Ground booking behavior in the current implementation, label published policy evidence separately from operational verification, and require first-party confirmation for event access, amenities, promotions and service commitments. Use helpful planning advice without invented customer experience.
