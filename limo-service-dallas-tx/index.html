Dallas Limo Service FAQs

Answers to common questions before you request a Dallas-Fort Worth ride.

What Dallas-Fort Worth areas do you serve?

Purple Heart Limo serves Dallas, Fort Worth, Arlington, Plano, Frisco, Irving, Grapevine, Southlake, McKinney, and surrounding DFW communities. Share your pickup and destination when requesting a quote so the trip can be confirmed.

Can I book car service to DFW Airport or Dallas Love Field?

Yes. Request airport transportation for DFW or Dallas Love Field and include your flight details, terminal, passenger count, and luggage needs. The team can confirm the pickup plan with your reservation.

Which vehicle should I choose for my group?

An executive sedan suits individual or small executive trips; an SUV adds room for passengers and bags. Stretch limousines, executive Sprinters, and party buses suit larger groups and celebrations. Include your group size and luggage in the request for a suitable recommendation.

How is a Dallas limo service quote calculated?

A quote depends on the vehicle, pickup and drop-off locations, date and time, trip length, number of stops, and service type. Ask for the total quote before booking; the fleet and rates pages provide additional starting-price guidance.

Can I arrange corporate or executive transportation?

Yes. Corporate and private car service can be requested for airport transfers, client pickups, meetings, roadshows, and multi-stop schedules. Provide the itinerary and passenger details so the right vehicle and timing can be arranged.

Do you provide wedding and prom transportation?

Yes. Request transportation for weddings, proms, and other special occasions with the ceremony or event times, pickup locations, passenger count, and any planned stops. Availability and vehicle options are confirmed with the booking.

How far ahead should I reserve a limo?

Reserve as early as possible for airport travel, weddings, proms, concerts, and busy weekends. Same-day availability may be limited, so call for time-sensitive requests.
