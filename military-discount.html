Discount Program

To every veteran and active duty service member in Texas — we salute you and we've got you covered. Because service to this country deserves to be honored, even when you're just trying to get to the airport.

10% Off for Those Who Served

Purple Heart Limo was founded by a U.S. military veteran. We know what it means to serve, and we believe in giving back to those who've served our country.

Active duty military, veterans, and their immediate families receive 10% off every ride. Just call us and mention your service — we'll take care of the rest. No complicated forms, no awkward verification processes.

Who Qualifies

✅ Active duty military (all branches)
✅ Veterans (honorably discharged)
✅ National Guard & Reserve members
✅ Retired military personnel
✅ Immediate family members of the above
