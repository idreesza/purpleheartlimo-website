### Internal Linking Strategy

**Linking Hierarchy**:

**Linking Targets**:
- Service pages: Link to 5-8 related pages
- Blog posts: Internal links in content (8-12 links)
- FAQ pages: Link to related services in answers

## ADVANCED OPTIMIZATION TACTICS

### Featured Snippet Optimization
**Target featured snippets with**:
- FAQs (Answer featured snippets)
- How-To (List featured snippets)
- Definitions (Definition featured snippets)
- Comparisons (Table featured snippets)

**FAQ Example**:
- Q: "What are TSA wait times at DFW?"
- A: "Current TSA wait times at DFW average [X] minutes. Peak hours are typically [times]. You can check real-time updates on [link]. For expedited service, consider [CTA]."

### Long-Tail Keyword Strategy
**Create content for**:
- 3-5 word keyword combinations (lower competition, high intent)
- Question-based keywords ("How to", "What is", "Where to")
- Feature-specific keywords ("Limo with hot tub", "Stretch Escalade limo")
- Seasonal keywords ("Prom limo rental", "Wedding limo service spring")

### Topical Cluster Strategy
**Create pillar content** (main service pages) with cluster content (blog posts supporting each pillar):

**Example Cluster**:
- Pillar: "Airport Limo Service" → Links to:
  - Blog: "DFW TSA Wait Times"
  - Blog: "Best Times to Travel"
  - Blog: "Ground Transportation Options"
  - FAQ: "Airport Transportation FAQs"

### User Intent Matching
**Match content to user intent**:
- **Informational** (blog posts) → Answer questions, provide value, build trust
- **Transactional** (service pages) → Clear benefits, pricing, CTAs, booking options
- **Navigational** (local pages) → Service areas, locations, contact info
- **Commercial** (landing pages) → Comparison, reviews, risk reversal, social proof
