Please resolve all Google Search Console indexing, routing, canonicalization, and technical SEO issues for purpleheartlimo.com.

### ISSUES TO RECTIFY
1. "Duplicate, Google chose different canonical than user": Google is detecting duplicate content versions because `.html` URLs (e.g., `/blog/dfw-family-activities.html`) exist alongside clean slug URLs (e.g., `/blog/dfw-family-activities`).
2. "Crawled - currently not indexed": Pages are being crawled but ignored due to non-canonical duplicates, missing meta descriptions, or improper self-referencing canonical tags.
3. "Blocked by robots.txt": Legitimate blog and core pages are blocked from crawling or incorrectly formatted.
4. "Not found (404)": Primary pages and legacy blog links are throwing 404 errors during Googlebot crawls.
5. Inconsistent URL Structures: Mixed presence of `.html` extensions, `www` vs non-`www` hostnames, double hyphens (`--`), and trailing dashes in slugs.

---

### ACTION PLAN FOR REPLIT

#### 1. Standardize Canonical URLs & Remove `.html` Extensions
- Strip `.html` extensions: Convert all routes to clean URLs (e.g., `/blog/dfw-family-activities` instead of `/blog/dfw-family-activities.html`).
- Implement 301 Permanent Redirects: Automatically redirect any incoming request ending in `.html` to its clean URL version.
- Enforce Single Domain: 301 redirect all `www.purpleheartlimo.com` traffic directly to `https://purpleheartlimo.com`.
- Dynamic Canonical Tags: Set a dynamic `<link rel="canonical" href="https://purpleheartlimo.com{clean_path}">` tag in the `<head>` of every page. Ensure it ALWAYS points to the clean, non-`.html` URL version.

#### 2. Slug Sanitization & Route Fixes
- Update slug creation logic so consecutive dashes (`--`) and trailing dashes (`-`) are cleanly stripped.
- Add 301 redirects mapping non-standard legacy slugs (e.g., `...in-austin--what-sets-us-apart`) to their cleaned canonical equivalents.
- Audit all core routes (`/about-us`, `/contact-us`, `/blog/:slug`) in the application router (React Router/wouter/Express) to ensure they render properly instead of returning 404s.
