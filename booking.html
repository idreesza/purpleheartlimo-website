Booking Your Ride With Purple Heart Limo

Reserving luxury transportation across Texas takes about two minutes. Tell us where you're going, choose the vehicle that fits your party, and we'll confirm your chauffeur by email and text. Whether it's an airport transfer at Austin-Bergstrom (AUS), DFW International, Dallas Love Field (DAL), or George Bush Intercontinental (IAH), a wedding, a corporate roadshow, prom night, or a night out, every reservation is backed by flat, upfront pricing — never surge fares.

How the booking process works

First, enter your pickup and drop-off locations so we can estimate your distance and quote a flat rate. Next, pick your date, time, and vehicle — from executive sedans and luxury SUVs to Sprinter vans, stretch limousines, and party buses. Finally, add your contact details and any special requests (child seats, meet-and-greet airport service, multiple stops, or hourly charter). You'll receive an instant request confirmation, and a dispatcher will finalize the details with you directly.

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book?
We recommend reserving at least 24–48 hours ahead for standard trips, and one to two weeks ahead for weddings, proms, and large group events during peak season. Same-day rides are often available — call us and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

Do you track flights?
Yes. For every airport pickup we monitor your flight in real time and adjust the chauffeur's arrival to match delays or early landings at no extra charge, including a complimentary grace period for waiting.

What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellations made more than 24 hours before pickup are fully refundable. See our cancellation policy for full details on late changes and refunds.

Which areas do you serve?
Purple Heart Limo is a Texan-owned company serving Austin, the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex, and Houston, along with surrounding suburbs such as Plano, Frisco, Irving, McKinney, Arlington, Highland Park, and University Park. Browse our service areas to confirm coverage for your route.

How do I pay and is pricing really flat?
You'll receive a clear, all-inclusive quote before you confirm — no hidden fees and no surge pricing. Payment is handled securely once your reservation is finalized with our team.
